Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 10, 2026

1. Overview

Aeroindia ("we," "our," "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy details how we handle information gathered from our corporate website, communication channels, and telemetry/data generated by our UAV platforms (such as RAKSHAK and NIRMAL) during official demonstrations or custom pilot programs.

2. Drone Sensor & Flight Data Policy

In our custom UAV development and precision agriculture pilot programs:

  • Visual Data: Camera feeds and imaging payloads used during agricultural spraying or facade inspection are stored locally on the ground control unit (GCS) and are not uploaded to public cloud servers without written consent.
  • Telemetry Logs: Flight trajectories, GPS coordinates, altitude details, and battery cycles are collected solely for system performance tracking and product safety research.

3. Website Inquiries & Information Collection

When you request a demo or submit inquiries on our website, we collect your name, email address, phone number, and institutional/company details. This data is used exclusively to evaluate partnership opportunities and coordinate live demonstrations.

4. Security Governance

We execute standard technical and organizational security controls to protect your data from unauthorized access, disruption, or leakage. Physical components (like flight logs stored on-board Pixhawk units) are handled in accordance with local aerospace security guidelines in India.

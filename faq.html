Answers regarding drone configurations, startup operations, agricultural capabilities, and candidate registration.

Who funds and supports Aeroindia?
Aeroindia is incubated and supported by the RSCOE Innovation & Incubation Foundation. The startup was awarded ₹50,000 in early-stage seed funding to develop and field-demonstrate our RAKSHAK drone prototypes.
What are the operational parameters of the RAKSHAK drone?
RAKSHAK is an autonomous agricultural spraying drone. It features a 20-liter payload tank, RTK centimeter-level GPS navigation, variable nozzle spray patterns, and an acoustic/light deterrent system to safely ward off birds and wild animals from agricultural fields.
Is the NIRMAL facade cleaning system fully autonomous?
NIRMAL is currently an award-winning industrial prototype. It utilizes custom laser-distance sensors and altitude hold firmware to hover safely near vertical glass surfaces. While it supports pre-programmed vertical cleaning trajectories, it is monitored by a remote safety pilot during high-altitude runs.
Who can participate in the UDAAN 2026 workshop?
UDAAN 2026 is designed for engineering undergraduates, diploma students, researchers, and UAV hobbyists. No prior coding or assembly experience is required. The curriculum begins with structural mechanics and proceeds to flight telemetry scripting.
How does Aeroindia manage airspace clearances in India?
All flight operations strictly comply with DGCA Drone Rules, 2021. Prior to flying, our pilots inspect airspace zones (Green, Yellow, Red) on the government DigitalSky portal and submit automated flight plans. We do not operate drones in Yellow or Red zones without corresponding defense or civilian command clearances.
How can we coordinate for custom UAV engineering?
Institutions or industries requiring bespoke flight controllers, mechanical mounts, or specific sensor payloads can contact our engineering team directly via the contact form or email. We follow a detailed Discovery, design, field testing, and certification handover cycle.

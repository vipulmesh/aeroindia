About Aeroindia

OUR
STORY

Founded in 2023, Aeroindia is a student-led UAV innovation company on a mission to put Indian drone technology on the world stage.

01 — Origin

Born from a belief that India could build better.

Aeroindia was founded in 2023 by a group of engineering students at RSCOE, Pune, united by a single conviction: that the solutions India needs can be engineered in India, for India.

What started as a project submission became a company. Our first prototype, RAKSHAK, addressed a real problem faced by millions of Indian farmers — the manual, hazardous process of crop spraying. The idea was simple: automate it, make it precise, make it affordable.

RSCOE's Innovation & Incubation Foundation believed in us, awarding ₹50,000 in seed funding. That validation changed everything. We stopped thinking like students and started thinking like engineers with a mission.

"Every flight path we design advances the ambition to put Indian drone technology on the global stage."

Aeroindia, 2023

02 — Foundation

Mission, Vision & Values

Mission

Build. Fly. Serve India.

Engineer sustainable, mission-ready UAV systems — made in India — for farmers protecting crops, medics delivering supplies, responders surveying disaster zones, and industries monitoring critical infrastructure.

Vision

India's aerial future, engineered here.

To be the leading Make-in-India drone company — exporting autonomous UAV platforms that combine aerospace precision with innovation-first R&D, recognised on the global stage.

Values

Precision. Purpose. Resilience.

  • — Engineering-first: every decision is driven by what works, not what impresses.
  • — Mission-aligned: we build for real problems faced by real people.
  • — Made in India: design, build, and iterate locally — for global impact.

03 — Trajectory

Our Timeline

2023

Aeroindia Founded

A group of RSCOE engineering students formalise a shared vision: to build drone solutions for India's most urgent problems. The company is born.

2024

RAKSHAK Development & ₹50,000 RSCOE Funding

RAKSHAK — our autonomous agricultural spraying drone — moves from concept to prototype. RSCOE's Innovation & Incubation Foundation awards Aeroindia ₹50,000 in seed capital, validating our approach.

₹50K Seed Funding RAKSHAK v1.0
2025

NIRMAL Wins Emerging Innovation Award

NIRMAL, our high-rise window-cleaning drone, wins the Emerging Innovation Award at Yuvahan 2025 and is exhibited at the CII NexGen Mobility Show — placing Aeroindia on India's national innovation map.

Emerging Innovation Award CII NexGen Mobility Show
2026

UDAAN 2026 — Training India's Drone Workforce

Aeroindia launches UDAAN 2026, a hands-on drone engineering workshop for the next generation of Indian aerospace engineers — combining build sessions, autonomous flight demonstrations, and industry mentorship.

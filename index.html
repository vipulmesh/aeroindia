01 — Mission

India's future in drone innovation starts here.

Aeroindia engineers sustainable, mission-ready UAV systems made in India — for farmers protecting crops, medics delivering supplies, responders surveying disaster zones, and industries monitoring critical infrastructure.

We combine aerospace precision with innovation-first R&D. Every flight path we design advances autonomous navigation, payload engineering, and the bold ambition to put Indian drone technology on the global stage.
